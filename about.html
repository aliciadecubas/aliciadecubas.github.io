A LITTLE CONTEXT

Hi, I'm Alicia

Game developer focused on gameplay, systems, and interactive experiences.

The short version

I started my university path in architecture, but eventually moved into game development after realizing how much I enjoyed the creative and technical side of building interactive experiences. What pushed me toward this field was my brother, who introduced me to games from a young age and inspired me to explore game development more deeply.

I’m especially interested in gameplay, audio, and graphics work, and I’m still figuring out exactly which area I want to focus on next. Outside of work, I enjoy reading, playing games, and listening to music. I’m especially drawn to cozy, simulation-style games like Stardew Valley and Coral Island, because they balance accessible mechanics with a strong sense of atmosphere and player engagement.

What I enjoy

  • Gameplay programming
  • Audio implementation
  • Graphics programming
  • Collaborative development
  • Prototyping ideas

How I work

I approach projects by validating the core idea quickly, then building the systems that make it feel solid, readable, and fun to play. I value clear communication, close collaboration with the team, and iterative development, refining mechanics and presentation until the final experience feels cohesive and intentional.
